One Minute Testamony

I was once a Chinese pagan who longed to be a winner, yet repeatedly experienced failure. My former faith collapsed after the 1989 Tiananmen massacre. My career later collapsed when I was laid off in 2001, and by 2003 my family was on the verge of breaking apart.

When I was about to totally collapse, a verse I had long rejected—Genesis 3:7—suddenly spoke to me and reminded me that I was created by God. I am His child, not merely the product of blind evolution. That realization became the turning point that transformed both my faith and my life.

Since then, I have found a new identity and lasting hope through the cross of Jesus Christ. His glory has become my glory, and His victory my victory. Today, I give thanks not only to my Savior, but also to the many Christians, churches, American believers, and this country that God has used to guide and sustain me on this journey
