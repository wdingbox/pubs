I was once a Chinese pagan who always wanted to be a successful man, but I kept experiencing failure. My former faith collapsed after the 1989 Tiananmen massacre. Later, my career collapsed when I was laid off in 2001, and by 2003 my family was on the verge of breaking apart.

When I was about to completely collapse, a verse I had rejected for a long time—Genesis 3:7— suddenly spoke to me. It reminded me that I was created by God. I am His child, not just the product of evolution from monkeys. That realization became the turning point that changed both my faith and my life.

Since then, I have found a new identity and lasting hope through the cross of Jesus Christ. His glory has become my glory, and His victory my victory. And finally, I want to thank not only my Savior, but also the many American and Chinese Christians, churches, and this country that has accepted me as an immigrant.
